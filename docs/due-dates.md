# Due dates and reminders

## Giving a note a due date

With the area at the bottom expanded, tap the **alarm clock** button while adding or editing a note. Choose a date and time, then **Set Due Date**. To remove it, open it again and choose **Clear Due Date**.

You can also tap the date under a note's buttons in the list to set, change or clear its due date.

A [long-form note](long-form.md) has its own alarm clock button at the bottom of its editor, next to Cancel and Finish (after the share button, for handwriting). It works once the title has been accepted, and the due date is saved as soon as you set it. Turning a note into a long-form note keeps its due date.

In the list, a note with a due date shows "Due" and the date under its buttons, instead of when it was created ("Cr."):

- in black (not grey) while it's still to come, and **bold** in its last week;
- in **bold red** once it has passed.

## Reminders

When a due time arrives, the app can send a notification to your devices, saying "Due:" and the note's title.

To turn this on for a device, go to **Settings**, then **Reminders**, and tap **Turn on**. Your browser will ask whether to allow notifications: say yes. **Send test** checks it works.

Turn it on on each device you'd like reminders on. On an Android phone or tablet, reminders work best with the app installed (see [Offline use and installing the app](offline.md)).
