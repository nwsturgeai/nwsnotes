# Getting started with nwsnotes

## Getting an account

Go to the [sign-up page](/signup) (or, on the login page, choose **No account yet? Ask for one**). Fill in your email address, your name and a password you choose (at least 10 characters), then **Ask for an account**. Once your account has been confirmed, you'll get an email with the link to log in: use your email and that password. Until then, logging in says it's still waiting to be confirmed.

Once you've logged in, you're straight into the main app screen.  At the bottom is an area to type a new note.  Clicking ENTER saves the note, or you can click the "+" button.

In the top-right, there is a cog icon.  Clicking this takes you to the Settings page.

## Tags

Notes are grouped and organised by Tags.  You define what Tags are available (mostly), and you choose which notes are tagged with which Tags.

Tags themselves are grouped into Categories.  Categories are mostly an advanced topic, to start with you need to have at least one Category to put Tags into.

You might want to group your notes by what aspect of your life they relate to i.e. what type of note each one is.  You could create a Category called "Type", and within it create Tags e.g. "Home", "Work", "Shopping".  You should find it easy to create a Category and Tags within the Settings page.

On the Notes page, just below where you can type a new note, you will see your available Tags.  You can click those Tag pills to set the Tags on a note before you create it.

Each note in your list has a quick button to edit it.  Editing happens in the same place as where you add new notes.  When editing a note, you can change its Tags by clicking on different Tag pills.  Click the tick mark button to save your edits.

### Just a little more advanced - simple filtering by Tags:

There are drop-downs at the top of the Notes page that let you filter which notes you are looking at according to which Tags are set on each note.  So for example you could easily filter your list to just see your Shopping notes.  Whilst your list is filtered, any new notes you add automatically get the Tags that put them into this list - in our example, new notes would automatically be tagged with the Shopping tag.

## Crossing out and deleting notes

After you create a note, it appears in your notes list, tagged as "New" to show it has been recently created.

Alongside the edit button, each note has 2 other buttons:
- The hourglass button marks the note as "Waiting".
- The filing cabinet button marks the note as "Archived".

You can choose when to set notes to Waiting or Archived as you like.  I think of Archived notes like notes that are crossed out on a paper to-do list.

Once notes have been archived, you can choose when to remove them from your notes lists.

To delete archived notes in your current tab, click the notes-ordering button just under the settings cog in the top-right.  Then a button saying "Archived -> Bin" should appear, which you can click.
