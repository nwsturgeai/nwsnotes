# Google Calendar

nwsnotes can keep your notes with a due date in Google Calendar, and turn events you add there into notes. Changes go both ways: rename a note and its event is renamed, move an event and its note's due date moves.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Google Calendar**, and tap **Connect Google Calendar**.
2. Google asks which account to use, and whether nwsnotes may use your calendar. As nwsnotes isn't checked by Google, it first says "Google hasn't verified this app": tap **Advanced**, then **Go to nwsnotes**. Then tap **Allow**.
3. You're back in Settings, which says **Connected**.

nwsnotes makes a new calendar called **nwsnotes** in your Google account. Only that calendar is synced: your other calendars, and their meetings and birthdays, are left alone. Each person connects their own Google account.

Your notes that are due in the future appear in it straight away. Ones that are already overdue don't, so the calendar isn't filled with old notes.

## Your notes in Google Calendar

A note appears as a 15-minute event at its due time, while it's New, Active or Waiting. Archiving it, putting it in the Bin or clearing its due date removes the event. Bringing the note back puts the event back.

- **The title** is the note's title. A handwritten note shows as "Handwritten note".
- **The description** starts with the note's Tags as hashtags, such as `#Home #Shopping`. A long-form note's details come after them. If the details are handwritten, it says "Handwritten description available in nwsnotes".
- **The alert** comes from Google Calendar, at the due time, instead of nwsnotes' own reminder.

Changes reach Google within a few seconds.

## Adding notes from Google Calendar

Add an event to the **nwsnotes** calendar, on your phone or computer, and it becomes a New note, due when the event starts.

- **Tags:** put hashtags on the first line of the description, or anywhere in the title, such as `Oat milk #Shopping`. Use an underscore for a space: `#Garden_ideas` for the Tag "Garden ideas". Upper or lower case doesn't matter. Hashtags in the title are moved to the description.
- **Details:** anything else in the description makes it a long-form note, with that text as its details. Bold, links and other formatting are left out.
- **All-day events** are due at 20:00 on their day (the first day, if they last longer), and show as "all day" in your notes. They stay all-day in Google unless you give the note a different time.
- **Repeating events** become a note with no due date.

Changes usually reach nwsnotes within a few seconds, and at most about five minutes.

## Changing and deleting in Google

- Renaming an event renames its note (but a handwritten note keeps its handwriting).
- Moving an event changes the note's due date.
- Adding or removing hashtags adds or removes the note's Tags. A hashtag that doesn't match a Tag, such as a typo, is left as it is and does nothing.
- Writing a description for an ordinary note makes it a long-form note.
- **Deleting an event puts its note in the Bin**, so you can still get it back.

Each change from Google shows in your [History](history.md), marked "from Google Calendar", and can be undone like any other.

## If something goes wrong

If you remove nwsnotes' access in your Google account, or delete the nwsnotes calendar, syncing stops and Settings says why. Tap **Connect again** to carry on (deleting the calendar means a new one is made).

To stop syncing, tap **Disconnect** in Settings. The nwsnotes calendar stays in your Google account; you can delete it there if you no longer want it.
