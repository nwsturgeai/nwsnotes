# Handwriting with a stylus

If you have a tablet with a pen (stylus), you can write notes by hand instead of typing them.

## Stylus mode

Switch on **Stylus mode** in Settings. A small signature icon at the top of the screen shows it's on. Then:

- the box for a new note becomes a strip to write on with your pen;
- **+** adds what you've written as a note, and **Clear** wipes it;
- only the pen (or a mouse) writes, so a hand resting on the screen doesn't leave marks or press buttons.

Stylus mode is remembered on each device, so it can be on for the tablet and off for your phone.

## Pen thickness

Choose from three thicknesses in **Settings** (the Pen section, shown while stylus mode is on), or with the buttons at the bottom of a handwritten long-form note.

## Long-form notes by hand

In stylus mode, a new [long-form note](long-form.md) has a whole page to write on. Along the bottom:

- the **eraser** button: while it's on, the pen rubs out instead of writing (the end of the pen may work as an eraser too);
- the **pen thickness** buttons;
- the **share** button, which saves the page as a picture, or sends it to another app.

## Editing handwritten notes

Tap a handwritten note's pen to add to it, or Clear it and start again. There's no way to turn handwriting into typed text.
