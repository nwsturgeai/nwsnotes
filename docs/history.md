# History: going back

The app keeps a record of your last 500 changes from the past 30 days: notes added, edited, archived or deleted, Categories and tabs changed, the order of your notes, and so on.

## Undo

Straight after you change a note on the notes page (add, edit, archive, reorder and so on), an **Undo** button appears at the top, just left of the Settings cog. Tap it to undo that change. It then becomes **Redo**, in case you change your mind.

The button stays for 5 minutes after each change. It only undoes the one latest change you made on that page; to go back further, use the History. It isn't there while you're offline, and it's greyed out while you're editing a note.

If the note has been changed since (on another device, say), Undo can't be used, and says so: use the History instead.

## Going back

In **Settings**, tap **History**. The changes are listed, newest first. Tap **Go back to here** on one, and everything you changed after it is undone, as if those later changes had never happened.

Going back is itself a change in the History, so if you go back too far, you can undo that too.

## Backups

For a copy you keep yourself, use **Download backup** in Settings.
