# Lifetime: New, Active, Waiting, Archived and Bin

Every note has a **Lifetime**, which says where it is in its life. It's shown by the note's background colour, and by a "New" pill.

| Lifetime | What it means | How it looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Just added | A "New" pill |
| **Active** | Ongoing | Plain |
| **Waiting** | Waiting for something or someone | A different background |
| **Archived** | Done: crossed off | Dark grey |
| **Bin** | To be deleted | Shown only if a filter asks for it |

## Changing a note's Lifetime

Each note has buttons on its right:

- the **hourglass** marks it as Waiting;
- the **filing cabinet** archives it;
- once archived, a **bin** button moves it to the Bin;
- the **curved arrow** brings a Waiting, Archived or binned note back to Active.

## Tidying up

In **Settings**, under **Lifetime**:

- **Reset New** makes every New note Active. You can also tap any "New" pill to do this.
- **Archived to Bin** moves every archived note to the Bin.
- **Empty Bin** deletes the notes in the Bin for good. The app asks first.

Even after emptying the Bin, you can get notes back with [History](history.md), for up to 30 days (as long as they are within your last 500 changes).
