# Long-form notes

Most notes are a single line. A **long-form note** also has a page of details, for anything longer: plans, minutes of a meeting, a recipe.

## Creating one

With the area at the bottom expanded, tap the **page** button (under the + button). The long-form editor opens over your notes:

1. Type a title and tap the **tick** next to it (or press Enter).
2. Choose its Tags in the band below the title. The **tags** button at the right of that band picks the current tab's Tags for you.
3. Write the details in the big box below.
4. Tap **Finish** (the tick at the bottom right) when you're done, or press Ctrl+Enter.

The details are saved by themselves as you go, so nothing is lost if you're interrupted. **Cancel** (the X, only while creating a new one) deletes the note, asking first if you've written anything.

The details can use the same formatting as titles: `*bold*`, `_underline_` and `/italic/`.

## Opening it again

In the list, a long-form note shows its title and an amber "Long Form" pill. Tap its pen to open it again.

## Notes that belong to it

Any note can belong to a long-form note, its **parent**, and that includes other long-form notes, so you can build a tree: "Holiday plans", with "Flight options" inside it, and notes inside that.

- A note that belongs to a long-form note has a purple pill with the parent's title.
- A long-form note with notes inside it has a purple pill showing a small diagram and how many. If the tab you're on hides some of them, it says so, for example "2 of 3". Tap it to list just that note and everything inside it.

There are three ways to put a note inside a long-form note:

- **While a long-form note is open**, any notes you add in the area at the bottom belong to it. It's a handy way to jot down the actions from a meeting while writing up the minutes.
- **The Parent line:** with the area at the bottom expanded, the last line under the Tags is **Parent**. Tap its pill to choose a parent for the note you're adding or editing, or to take it out of one. Notes you add keep the same parent until you change it.
- **In the long-form editor,** the band of Tags has the same **Parent** pill, for the long-form note itself.

The pop-up lists every long-form note that isn't in the Bin, each with the long-form notes inside it indented below. The buttons at the top sort them in **Note order** (as in your list) or **Alphabetical**; notes inside another stay under it either way. Tap the small triangle left of a note to hide the notes inside it, and again to show them. Long-form notes that are Waiting or Archived say so in a small grey pill after their title. To find one quickly, open **Filter by tag** and pick Tags, with pills or dropdowns as in the area at the bottom. Only long-form notes with those Tags can be ticked; the notes they're inside are shown greyed, so you can see where they are. Tick one, or none, and tap **Set Parent**. A note can't go inside itself or inside a note that's inside it, and a note can only go inside a long-form note belonging to the same person.

If a parent note is deleted by emptying the Bin, the notes that were inside it no longer have a parent. Going back to before that in the History puts them back inside it.

## Turning a note into a long-form note

While editing an ordinary typed note with the area expanded, tap the **expand** button (four arrows pointing outwards) to give it a page of details. A note that belongs to a long-form note keeps its parent.

## Handwritten details

In stylus mode, the details are a page to write on instead. See [Handwriting with a stylus](handwriting.md).
