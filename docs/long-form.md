# Long-form notes

Most notes are a single line. A **long-form note** also has a page of details, for anything longer: plans, minutes of a meeting, a recipe.

## Creating one

With the area at the bottom expanded, tap the **page** button (under the + button). The long-form editor opens over your notes:

1. Type a title and tap the **tick** next to it (or press Enter).
2. Choose its Tags in the band below the title. The **tags** button at the right of that band picks the current tab's Tags for you.
3. Write the details in the big box below.
4. Tap **Finish** (the tick at the bottom right) when you're done, or press Ctrl+Enter.

The details are saved by themselves as you go, so nothing is lost if you're interrupted. **Cancel** (the X, only while creating a new one) deletes the note, asking first if you've written anything.

The details can use the same formatting as titles: `*bold*`, `_underline_` and `/italic/`.

## Opening it again

In the list, a long-form note shows its title and an amber "Long Form" pill. Tap its pen to open it again.

## Notes that belong to it

While a long-form note is open, any notes you add in the area at the bottom belong to it, and get an amber pill with its title. It's a handy way to jot down the actions from a meeting while writing up the minutes.

## Turning a note into a long-form note

While editing an ordinary typed note with the area expanded, tap the **expand** button (four arrows pointing outwards) to give it a page of details.

## Handwritten details

In stylus mode, the details are a page to write on instead. See [Handwriting with a stylus](handwriting.md).
