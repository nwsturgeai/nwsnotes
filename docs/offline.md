# Offline use and installing the app

## Installing it on an Android phone or tablet

Open the app in Chrome and log in, then open Chrome's menu (the three dots) and choose **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It then opens from its own icon like any other app, full screen, and works best for reminders and offline use.

iPhones and iPads aren't supported yet.

## Without a connection

The app keeps a copy of your notes on the device, so you can still read them, add notes, and edit or archive them with no connection. A small **cloud with a line through it** at the top shows you're offline, and says how many changes are waiting. They're sent by themselves as soon as the connection is back.

Some things need the server, so they're greyed out while offline: Settings, putting notes in order, and changing several notes' Tags at once.

## On several devices

Your notes are the same on all your devices. A change on one shows up on the others within moments.

If you edit a note that has been changed somewhere else since you started (in another tab, on another device, or by someone you share it with), the app tells you instead of quietly replacing that change. You can then keep your version or keep the other one.

Tabs in the same browser share your login: logging out in one logs out the others. Changes made in a tab that has lost its connection are sent by any other tab that still has one.
