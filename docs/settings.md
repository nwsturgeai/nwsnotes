# Settings

Tap the **cog** at the top right to open Settings.

## Remembered on each device

These are kept separately on each of your devices, so your phone and tablet can differ:

- **Stylus mode:** write notes with a pen. See [Handwriting with a stylus](handwriting.md).
- **Columns:** how the notes are laid out on wider screens. **Single** is always one column. **Wide**, **Medium** and **Narrow** fit as many columns as the screen has room for, at least 500, 400 or 300 pixels wide each.
- **Tab counts:** show each tab's number of notes. See [Tabs](tabs.md).
- **Pen:** the pen's thickness, while stylus mode is on.

## For your notes

- **History:** go back to how things were. See [History](history.md).
- **Lifetime:** Reset New, Archived to Bin and Empty Bin. See [Lifetime](lifetime.md).
- **Categories** and **Tabs:** see [Tags and Categories](tags-and-categories.md) and [Tabs](tabs.md).
- **Reminders:** see [Due dates and reminders](due-dates.md).
- **Text messages:** link your phone, to add notes by text, even by voice from the car. See [Texting notes in, from the car](text-messages.md).
- **Backup:** **Download backup** saves a copy of all your notes, Categories and tabs to a file.

## Your account

- **Change name:** the name shown in Settings, and to anyone you share tags with.
- **Change email:** the email you log in with. Enter the new one and your current password, and we'll email a link to the new address. Your email changes only once you open the link and press **Confirm** (it works for 24 hours), so a mistyped address changes nothing. Your old address is then emailed to say so. You stay logged in on your devices.
- **Change password:** choose your own password. Your other devices are logged out, and you log in there again with the new one. You're emailed to say it was changed, so you'd know if someone else did it.
- **Log out.**

Each person's notes are completely separate: nobody else can see yours.

## App version

Shows which version you're running, with **Check for updates**. When a new version is ready, a notice offers **Reload** to switch to it.

Settings need a connection to the server, so they aren't available while you're offline.
