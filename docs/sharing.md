# Sharing Tags with other people

You can share any of your Tags with other people who use nwsnotes. They then see your notes with that Tag, and can work on them as far as you allow. The notes stay yours.

## Sharing a Tag

1. In **Settings**, tap the Category the Tag is in.
2. Tap the **share** button (three joined dots) beside the Tag. A Tag has to be saved before it can be shared.
3. Choose who to share it with. People you've shared with before, and people who have shared with you, are in the list. For anyone else, type the email address they log in with.
4. For each person, choose what they may do:
   - **Read-only:** they can see the notes, but not change them.
   - **Edit:** they can also change the notes' titles and details, add new notes with the Tag, and use Waiting, Archive and Reinstate.
   - **Edit and delete:** they can also move notes to the Bin.
5. Tap **Save**. It takes effect straight away.

Open the same dialog to change what someone may do, or tap the **×** beside them to stop sharing with them.

## Seeing what you've shared

On your notes, a Tag you've shared has a small **people** icon after its name, showing what the people you've shared it with can do. If anyone can edit and delete, it has a **trash can** on its corner; otherwise, if anyone can edit, a **pencil**; if everyone can only read, it's just the people.

A [Tab](tabs.md) that shows one of your shared Tags has the same icon after its name, so you can tell which Tabs show notes other people can see.

The same icon shows after the Tag's name in most other places it's listed: the Category lists in the filter row, a Tab's page and a long-form note; the tag pills under the new-note box (with **Show all tags** on) and when selecting several notes; the lists of Categories and Tabs in Settings; and beside the Tag on its Category's page. The tag dropdowns under the new-note box (with **Show all tags** off) don't show it.

## What they see, and what they can't do

- They see the whole of each note with that Tag, so only share a Tag whose notes you're happy for them to read.
- They don't see your other Tags on those notes.
- Nobody you share with can change a note's Tags or its due date, or empty your Bin. Only you can.
- Notes they add with the Tag are yours: they're in your notebook, and count towards your note limit.
- Their changes show in your [History](history.md), marked with their name, so you can go back past them.
- If you delete the Tag, or take it off a note, those notes are no longer shared.

## Tags shared with you

When someone shares a Tag with you, a Category called **Shared with me** appears, holding every Tag shared with you. Beside each Tag is the name of the person who shared it, for example "Shopping (Pat)".

- You can filter on these Tags, and use them in your own [Tabs](tabs.md).
- To add a note to someone else's Tag (if they allow it), choose that Tag for the new note, or add it on a Tab that shows that Tag. Such a note can't also have your own Tags.
- Notes you can only read have an eye where their buttons would be.
- Tap **Shared with me** in Settings to choose the order and colours of its Tags. These only change how the notes look to you.
- You can put shared notes in your own order, without changing the owner's order.
- [Texting a note](text-messages.md) to a Tab of a Tag shared with you adds it to that Tag, as long as the Tab shows just one shared Tag.
