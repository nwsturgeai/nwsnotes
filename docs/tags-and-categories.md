# Tags and Categories

Tags are how notes are organised. Every Tag belongs to a **Category**, such as a "Type" Category with the Tags "Home", "Work" and "Shopping".

## Setting up Categories and Tags

In **Settings** (the cog at the top right), the **Categories** section lists your Categories with their Tags.

- **Add category** creates a new one. Give it a name and at least one Tag, then **Save**.
- Tap a Category to rename it or its Tags, add Tags, or delete Tags. Deleting a Tag takes it off every note that had it (the app asks first).
- **Multi-select:** tick this if a note may have several Tags from this Category at once (say, a "People" Category). Leave it unticked if a note should have just one (say, "Priority": High, Medium or Low).

## Putting things in order

The order of your Categories, and of the Tags within each, is the order they appear everywhere in the app.

- In Settings, drag a Category or Tab by its **grip** (the six dots at its left), or use the up and down arrows.
- On a Category's page, drag its Tags the same way, then **Save**.

## Colours

A Tag can have a colour. On a Category's page, tap the small box next to a Tag's name and pick a colour. Notes with that Tag are then tinted with it, which makes them easy to spot. If a note has several coloured Tags, the first one (in Category order) wins.

## The Lifetime Category

There's one Category you don't create yourself: **Lifetime**. It records where each note is in its life: New, Active, Waiting, Archived or in the Bin. See [Lifetime](lifetime.md).

## Sharing

You can share a Tag with other people who use nwsnotes, and Tags others share with you appear in a Category called **Shared with me**. See [Sharing Tags with other people](sharing.md).
