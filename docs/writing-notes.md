# Writing and editing notes

## Adding a note

Type a title in the box at the bottom of the screen and press Enter, or tap the **+** button. The note goes to the bottom of the list, tagged with whatever Tags you've picked below the box.

The area at the bottom can be **expanded** or **collapsed** with the small round arrow button on the line above it:

- **Expanded** (the usual), every Tag shows as a pill you can tap.
- **Collapsed**, the area is smaller and each Category has a dropdown instead, leaving more room for your notes.

The app remembers which you prefer on each device.

## Adding a note at the top

Sometimes a new note belongs at the top of the list. Either:

- press **Ctrl+Enter** instead of Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac), or
- **hold** the + button for a second. A ring fills around it, then bursts outwards to show it's ready; let go and the note goes to the top.

## Bold, underline and italic

Note titles can have a little formatting:

| Type this | To get |
| --- | --- |
| `*like this*` | **bold** |
| `_like this_` | underlined |
| `/like this/` | *italic* |

The markers disappear in the list. They only count around whole words, so things like "and/or" or a date like 12/11/2026 are left alone.

## Editing a note

Tap the **pen** button on a note. It opens in the area at the bottom, where you can change its title and Tags. Tap the **tick** button to save, or the **X** (or press Escape) to cancel.

While you're editing, the tabs and the other notes' buttons are paused, so nothing changes under you.

## Columns

On a wider screen, notes can be shown in several columns. See [Settings](settings.md).
